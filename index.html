The Approach

How I would actually work with you

For Rising from The Ashes, I wouldn’t start by spending money (without proper plan) at ads and hoping the book reaches people globally. I’d start with what you already have: your audience of around 60,000 followers, your strong LinkedIn presence, the Cameroon radio interview, the Zimbabwean press coverage, and your November 14 launch in Kidderminster. My first step would be to build a dedicated landing page for your book, with your story, purchase links, the charity commitment, and an email sign up. I’d install the Meta Pixel and, where practical, the Conversions API, alongside Google Analytics, so I can track where visitors come from, what content attracts them, and which campaigns generate sales or enquiries. I’d then test small, focused Facebook and Instagram ads targeting readers in the UK and Nigeria before expanding towards Zimbabwean and African diaspora communities, personal development readers, churches, schools and universities. Rather than spending heavily on strangers, I’d retarget people who have visited your page, watched your trailer or engaged with your content. Since you earn around £2 to £3 per book, I’d be careful with the budget and make sure we are not spending more to acquire a buyer than the sale can reasonably support. The goal is to combine book sales with opportunities for interviews, speaking engagements, educational partnerships and wider recognition.

Over the three months, I’d use the first phase to build the landing page, produce a compelling book trailer, set up tracking and start collecting email subscribers. Around your November 14 launch, I’d amplify your strongest stories, media mentions and reader reactions, then use the results to refine the campaigns and reach more of the right people. I’d also build your email list with permission and use it to share reader stories, interviews, book updates and opportunities to engage with your message, rather than simply asking people to buy repeatedly. Alongside this, I’d actively pitch podcasts, radio stations, online publications, libraries, universities, churches and leadership programmes, keeping track of every pitch, response and feature. Through my work with Michael Vecchione on Hard Guys Cry, Teresa Michael and B.G. Howard, which you can explore through my portfolio at philipoladele.com, I’ve come to see book promotion as more than posting content. I want to build a system that keeps putting the book in front of the right people and gives us clear numbers to work with. I’d review performance weekly and report on reach, audience locations, email growth, media opportunities, reviews and sales wherever tracking allows. My aim is to help you build visibility that continues long after launch day, while making sure every pound we spend has a clear purpose.
