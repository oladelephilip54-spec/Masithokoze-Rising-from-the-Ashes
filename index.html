The Approach

How I would actually work with you

Landing page and tracking

I would start by building a dedicated landing page for the book. It will carry your story, purchase links, the charity commitment, and an email sign up. I will install the Meta Pixel and, where practical, the Conversions API, alongside Google Analytics, so we can track where visitors come from, what content attracts them, and which efforts lead to sales or enquiries.

Paid testing

I would then test small, focused Facebook and Instagram ads targeting readers in the UK and Nigeria before expanding towards Zimbabwean and African diaspora communities, personal development readers, churches, schools and universities. Rather than spending heavily on strangers, I would retarget people who have visited your page, watched the trailer or engaged with your content. Ad spend will be capped so we never risk more than the sale can reasonably support.

The November 14 launch

Around your November 14 launch in Kidderminster I would amplify your strongest stories, media mentions and reader reactions. The goal is to make the most of the momentum you already have and turn it into wider reach.

Media and outreach

Alongside the above I would actively pitch podcasts, radio stations, online publications, libraries, universities, churches and leadership programmes, keeping track of every pitch, response and feature. I want to build a system that keeps putting the book in front of the right people and gives us clear numbers to work with.
