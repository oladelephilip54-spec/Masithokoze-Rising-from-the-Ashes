How I would approach this

Start with what you already have

I wouldn’t begin by spending heavily on cold ads. I’d begin with your LinkedIn strength, the Cameroon radio interview, the Zimbabwean press coverage, and the 14 November launch.

1. Dedicated landing page + tracking

A clean page for the book with your story, purchase links, the charity commitment, and an email sign-up. Meta Pixel, Conversions API where practical, and Google Analytics so we can see exactly where people come from and what leads to a sale or enquiry.

2. Careful, focused advertising

Small tests on Facebook and Instagram aimed at UK and Nigerian readers first, then wider African diaspora, personal development audiences, churches, schools and universities. The main spend would go into retargeting people who already visited the page, watched the trailer, or engaged with your content. Given the margin, we stay disciplined.

3. Media and outreach

Active pitching to podcasts, radio, online publications, libraries, universities, churches and leadership programmes. Every pitch, response and feature is tracked so we know what’s working.

4. Email (3-month plan)

Build a list with permission and use it to share reader stories, interviews, updates and meaningful ways to engage with the book — not just repeated buy links.
